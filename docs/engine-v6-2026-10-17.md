# cx-spot-6 audit · futures positioning, gradient boosting, Momentum Rotation · 2026-10-17

## What changed after cx-spot-5

- **Futures positioning features.**
  - Six lagged features from Binance's public USDⓈ-M archives (`data.binance.vision`): open-interest change (1 and 7 days), retail long/short ratio, top-trader long/short ratio, taker buy/sell ratio, and the 3-day premium index (the driver of funding).
  - A probe confirmed that `fapi.binance.com` returns HTTP 451 for the publisher's GitHub runners, while the archives are reachable. Daily archives appear about 7–9 hours after the UTC day ends, so a decision uses only days that ended at least 36 hours earlier.
  - Training, replay and live scans share this rule. Missing data is imputed with the training mean and flagged.
- **Gradient boosting.** `lib/market/boosting.ts` implements histogram-split gradient-boosted trees with logistic loss and no dependency. On the purged holdout, each horizon chooses between logistic regression and boosting, together with an exit profile and minimum edge, under the same validation gate as before.
- **Two-year training.** 2024-07-19 → 2026-07-19 on twenty predeclared markets. The 90-day replay that follows is out-of-sample.
- **Momentum Rotation.** A portfolio strategy with a single parameter choice:
  - Ranks by risk-adjusted momentum, requiring positive momentum and a close above SMA50.
  - Uses inverse-volatility weights and a weekly rebalance.
  - Holds cash unless BTC closes above its 100-day average.
  - Lookback and holding count are chosen on the training window.
- **Bundle safety.** The full model (weights and trees) stays server-side. The browser loads a weight-free summary.

| 1H | 21,121 | logistic | 0.52 | −0.14R | 106 trades, −0.09R | Not validated |

Adding futures positioning and a second year did not make 15M, 1H or 4H predictable. 4H, which validated on the one-year window, no longer does. Only 1D passes, and its holdout sample (36 selected trades) is small.

## Out-of-sample replay · 2026-07-20 → 2026-10-17, 12 markets

Five trades cannot confirm or reject the 1D model. Neither system shows a statistically positive edge in this window.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Rotation beat BTC over two training years but lagged badly during the latest BTC-led rally, when altcoin leaders did not keep pace.

## Why cx-spot-6 ships with this model

The decision rule was set before the results were seen: train on the longer window with the richer features, then publish whatever passes the holdout gate. Choosing the one-year model because its replay looked better would use the out-of-sample window for selection, which would make the published evidence meaningless. The engine therefore publishes BUY plans only on 1D. They will be rare. Everything else stays research-only.

## Honest conclusion

Better tools did not unlock a reliable short-horizon edge from public candle and positioning data; that is a real finding, not a failure to try. What the system now does well:

- It refuses to trade where no edge was measured.
- It sizes and exits trades sensibly.
- It shows calibrated probabilities instead of a misleading score.
- It reports evidence honestly.

Higher, proven profitability needs forward-tracked evidence over more market regimes. Retrain monthly and judge 1D and Rotation on their live ledgers before committing real capital.
