# cx-spot-5 engine audit · 2026-10-17

## Why the engine changed

The cx-spot-4 replay (BTC/ETH/SOL/INJ, 90 days, 257 completed trades) won 22.6% of trades. 69% of trades hit their stop, only 5.4% reached the 4R TP3, and most pair/horizon accounts had a profit factor below 1. Trades that survived to the time exit were mostly profitable, which showed that the full-position 4R target gave back gains. The 0–100 score mostly measured EMA alignment and had never been tested as a predictor.

## What cx-spot-5 adds

- **Managed exits.** Sell 50% at TP1, move the remainder to a costed breakeven, trail it by a frozen ATR multiple, and close at TP3, the trail or the holding limit. Stops win ambiguous bars. Legacy plans are unchanged.
- **Market regime.** No BUY when the BTC higher-timeframe trend is bearish and fewer than 30% of scanned markets close above EMA50.
- **Calibrated edge.** An L2 logistic model over twenty causal features estimates win probability and expected R after costs. `scripts/train-model.ts` labels every long-entry candidate with the production fill and exit engine. It picks each horizon's exit profile (grid of 12) and minimum edge on a purged chronological holdout (the last 30% of training decisions), then refits on all training rows.
- **No edge, no trade.** A horizon publishes BUY only when its model passed the holdout: AUC ≥0.55, ≥15 selected trades, a positive 90% bootstrap lower bound on selected mean R, and selected mean R above the all-candidate mean. Horizons that fail stay research-only (WAIT).
- **Sizing and portfolio.** Risk is halved for top-quintile ATR, reduced to ¾ for BTC beta ≥1.5, and halved on 15M. Active simulations may risk at most 6% of initial capital together.
- **Evidence.** The replay now covers twelve predeclared markets with shared BTC/breadth context. It reports R multiples, pooled per-horizon expectancy with a bootstrap interval, Monte Carlo drawdown, exit reasons and R histograms.

- **4H:** 69 trades, −0.23R.

With the final no-edge-no-trade rule, only validated 4H plans publish BUY.

For the four markets shared with the cx-spot-4 report, the summed account returns across 16 pair/horizon accounts were:

- cx-spot-4: −53.6%
- cx-spot-5 with the model, measured before the no-edge-no-trade rule: −25.9%

That cx-spot-5 replay still included rule-based 1H/1D trades. Most of the improvement came from removing 15M and from the 4H model.

## Honest conclusion

cx-spot-5 cuts losses by trading only where earlier data showed a measurable edge. It does **not** yet show a statistically positive edge out-of-sample. The 4H result is roughly breakeven, and its 90% interval includes zero. Ninety days is one market period. These numbers are retrospective research, not a forecast or a guarantee. Retraining as new data arrives, and forward-tracking the public ledger, decide whether 4H holds up and whether other horizons ever validate.
